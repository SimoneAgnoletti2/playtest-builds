How to play

          
    
            
  • Each move shifts your head by one cell (arrows/WASD, swipe, tap on the grid or the buttons). Then the 3 rivals move.
    • 
            
  • Outside your colour you leave a trail; coming back you capture the trail and everything it encloses.
    • 
            
  • Points = cells × multiplier. The multiplier rises by 1 every 4 trail cells: long loops are worth more but are riskier. Gems are worth double.
    • 
            
  • If a rival steps on your trail, the land ends at once (points already scored stay). You can do the same to them.
    • 
            
  • Reach the target within the moves to go to the next land and pick a relic that bends the rules in your favour. 8 lands; the 4th and 8th are harder.
    • 
            
  • The Daily climb is the same for everyone: same maps, same relic offers (base ones only). The first attempt is the official one. Relics unlocked with stars appear in the Free climb.
